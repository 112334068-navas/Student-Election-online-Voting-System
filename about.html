General Election Rules

  • Only registered students are eligible to vote.
  • Each student can vote only once.
  • Voter ID and login credentials must not be shared.
  • Voting must be completed within the official election time.
  • Any fraudulent activity will result in disqualification.

Candidate Eligibility Rules

  • The candidate must be a currently enrolled student.
  • Minimum attendance requirement must be satisfied.
  • No disciplinary record in the current academic year.
  • Must submit nomination form before the deadline.

Voting Guidelines

  • Students must log in using their official student ID.
  • Review candidate details carefully before voting.
  • Vote submission is final and cannot be changed.
  • System automatically logs out after voting.
Important Notice: Any attempt to manipulate or hack the voting system will lead to strict disciplinary action as per institutional policies.
